# Reminders

Mina allows you to set reminders that will ping you via DM or announce in a Voice Channel.

## Usage

### Voice & Text Commands
- "!remind me to [text] in [time]"
- "Remind me to [text] in [time]"

### Slash Commands
- `/reminders list`: Show your active reminders.
- `/reminders cancel [id]`: Cancel a pending reminder.

### AI Tools
Mina's AI is now context-aware and can set reminders naturally:
- "Set a reminder for 10 minutes from now."
- "Remind me to wake up in 8 hours."

## Notifications
- **Voice Channel**: If you are in a voice channel when the timer expires, Mina will join (if needed) and speak the reminder.
- **DM**: If you are offline or not in voice, Mina will send you a Direct Message.
