# Notes

Mina can act as your personal notebook, letting you save text notes, ideas, or lists quickly via voice, text, or slash commands.

## Usage

### Voice & Text Commands
- "Make a note that [text]"
- "Take a note [text]"
- "!note [text]"

### Slash Commands
- `/note [content]`: Save a new note.
- `/notes`: List all your saved notes (includes IDs).
- `/delnote [id]`: Delete a note by its ID.

### AI Tools
You can also ask Mina naturally:
- "Write down that I need to buy milk."
- "What notes do you have for me?"
- "Delete the note about the car."

## Storage
Notes are stored persistently in `data/notes.json` and are unique to your User ID.
